Serving as Director of Technology and interim Creative Director at JUXT was a crucible in rapid creative technology execution. We operated as a tight, boutique SWAT team of fewer than twenty engineers, architects, and visual designers delivering bespoke physical installations and digital platforms.

## 01 // The Sensory & Physical Brief
Global brands (IBM, Cisco, Salesforce, Plantronics, and the NFL) needed unpaved interactive experiences that shattered conventional agency banner-ad playbooks: live arena keynote visuals, interactive spatial kiosks, and dynamic real-time data environments deployed under brutal event timelines.

## 02 // Bespoke Hardware & Algorithmic Stack
* **Pioneering "Co-Design in Code":** We completely eliminated the traditional, lossy design-to-engineering handoff. For interactive projects, visual designers sat side-by-side with developers inside live code, TouchDesigner nodes, and OpenGL shader pipelines—tuning typography, particle physics, motion curves, and layout states directly on running hardware in real time.
* **Full-Stack Physical-Digital Integration:** Architected backends connecting custom sensor inputs, DMX lighting controllers, and real-time WebSockets data feeds into live stage and exhibit displays.

## 03 // Live Deployment & What Broke
Shipping bespoke physical computing under tight event deadlines meant building rock-solid failovers. When running real-time installations for enterprise keynotes and stadium events, network drops, driver crashes, and stage vibration were constant threats. We engineered local offline caching, automated heartbeat watchdogs, and instantaneous hot-swapping failovers to ensure live arena screens never flickered or went dark.

## 04 // Breakthroughs, IP & Cultural Legacy
"Co-Design in Code" cut our concept-to-deployment cycle times by more than 50% while producing unmatched craft fidelity. The tight-knit, multi-disciplinary engineering culture we built at JUXT became my lifelong template for leading high-velocity creative technology and special projects teams.
