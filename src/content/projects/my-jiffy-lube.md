My Jiffy Lube was a massive enterprise platform modernization at AKQA, designed to transform vehicle maintenance and driver loyalty across 2,000+ independently operated service locations nationwide.

## 01 // The Production Frontier & Regulatory Stakes
Franchise operations represent one of the most hostile environments for software engineering: 2,000+ independent business owners, highly protective of regional financial data, operating on legacy on-premise servers tucked into physical service bay utility closets. The objective was to build real-time predictive vehicle maintenance and digital loyalty apps without corrupting legacy POS point-of-sale systems or violating regional franchise data isolation covenants.

## 02 // Systems Architecture & Data Plumbing
* **Distributed On-Prem to Cloud Sync:** Architected a resilient edge-to-cloud synchronization engine that scraped, normalized, and securely streamed maintenance records, vehicle mileage, and oil-life telemetry from localized closet servers to centralized cloud databases.
* **Multi-Tenant Data Isolation:** Engineered strict multi-tenant boundaries within Salesforce core cloud services, guaranteeing that regional franchise revenue and customer records remained segregated while corporate Shell leadership gained macro telemetry.
* **Predictive Maintenance Engine:** Built algorithms calculating dynamic oil degradation and service intervals based on driving patterns, triggering automated push notifications and localized digital coupons.

## 03 // The Engineering Crucible
The technical challenge was compounded by political stakes: because the platform was partially funded by regional franchise marketing co-ops, corporate could not mandate adoption. Franchisees were deeply loyal to traditional direct-mail paper coupons and hostile to digital changes. We had to build bulletproof offline resilience into the POS sync so that erratic bay internet never blocked customer checkout, while demonstrating to skeptical shop owners that mobile alerts drove measurable bay traffic.

## 04 // Hard Performance & Defensibility
The platform unified over 2,000 retail locations, processing millions of maintenance transactions and shifting consumer engagement from paper mailers to real-time predictive mobile maintenance. It established a modern digital footprint for Jiffy Lube and Shell, driving double-digit increases in repeat bay visits.
