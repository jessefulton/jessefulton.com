AKQA brought the iconic Ghost character out of the Destiny 2 video game world and into the smart home, delivering the first-ever integrated voice companion in a live gaming ecosystem.

### The Breakthrough Architecture
Built atop Amazon Alexa and Bungie's live player API network, the Destiny Ghost skill allowed players to speak directly to Ghost in real time:
- Asking for loadout swaps on the fly (*"Alexa, ask Ghost to equip my raid gear"*).
- Calling in clan reinforcements and querying real-time character level progressions.
- Exploring dynamic lore queries voiced by Hollywood actor Nolan North with procedural audio modulation.

### Intellectual Property & Honors
The technical breakthroughs developed during this project established new paradigms for asynchronous voice control in live stateful systems, leading to **6 US Patents** in interactive real-time systems architecture and multiple international honors including the **Cannes Silver Lion** and **Gold Clio**.
