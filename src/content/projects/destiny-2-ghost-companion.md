## Executive Context & Challenge

In 2017, voice interfaces were primarily transactional tools for checking weather or setting timers. Activision and Bungie wanted to break new ground for the launch of *Destiny 2* by creating an intelligent, in-universe companion that players could speak to naturally during live gameplay to manage loadouts, call for clan status, and explore dynamic lore.

## Architectural Mandate & Solution

As Director of Creative Technology at AKQA, led the end-to-end technical architecture, multimodal voice design, and API synchronization connecting Amazon Alexa with Bungie’s live game servers:

- **Sub-Second Bi-Directional State Sync:** Engineered real-time WebSocket and API bridging that synchronized player voice intents with dynamic live game state in under 800ms.
- **Dynamic In-Character Dialogue Engine:** Designed an intelligent conversational script tree voiced by Nolan North, allowing the Ghost to respond contextually based on the player's exact location, weapon inventory, and quest milestones.
- **Physical Wi-Fi Ghost Hardware:** Collaborated on the design and firmware integration for the custom limited-edition Wi-Fi-enabled Ghost physical device that pulsed with audio-synced LEDs.

## Recognition & Industry Impact

- Awarded **Cannes Gold Lion**, **Cannes Silver Lion**, and **Gold Clio Awards**.
- Resulted in **6 US Patents** in Human-Machine Interaction and Multimodal State Synchronization.
- Featured in a dedicated technical presentation at the **Game Developers Conference (GDC)**.
- Established the blueprint for how conversational AI companions interface with complex real-time software systems.
