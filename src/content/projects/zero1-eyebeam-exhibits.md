Throughout my graduate and post-graduate practice, I created software artworks, early WebGL browser experiments, and live projection-mapped audiovisual performances exploring the intersections of generative code, physical space, and digital subculture.

## 01 // The Sensory & Physical Brief
In the early 2010s, web graphics were transitioning from Flash to open GPU-accelerated standards (WebGL), while underground live performance was adopting real-time projection mapping. The goal was to take code out of academic sterile galleries and deploy it into chaotic, live public contexts: immersive festivals, warehouse raves, and browser-based net art hacks that subverted operating systems.

## 02 // Bespoke Hardware & Algorithmic Stack
* **WebGL Flying Toasters (The Creators Project SF):** Built an interactive browser-based tribute to the iconic *After Dark* flying toasters screensaver. Built using early WebGL and Web Audio APIs, the experience fully emulated a vintage Windows desktop environment—complete with the authentic Windows startup audio sequence, chiptune soundtrack, and clickable Notepad files exposing the underlying code.
* **Live Generative VJ Rigs (ZERO1 Biennial & Bay Area Shows):** Developed real-time generative visual pipelines using custom GLSL shaders, Max/MSP, and Syphon, projection-mapping reactive visuals onto non-standard 3D physical surfaces and stage architectures.

## 03 // Live Deployment & What Broke
Live VJing in underground venues and art festivals meant zero safety nets. At the ZERO1 Biennial in downtown San Jose, my collaborator James and I co-performed live visual sets for hundreds of attendees. The biggest battle was hardware stability: pushing early GPU shader pipelines without crashing the WebGL or OpenGL rendering contexts mid-track, while keeping projection rigs calibrated in hot, vibrating venue environments.

## 04 // Breakthroughs, IP & Cultural Legacy
* **The Creators Project SF (Intel & Vice):** Awarded **Honorable Mention** for WebGL Flying Toasters, recognized for technical execution and nostalgic cultural homage.
* **ZERO1 Biennial & Eyebeam NYC:** Established an early foundation in real-time generative performance, human perception, and spatial projection that directly fed into my subsequent interactive retail and spatial computing work at Nike and AKQA.
