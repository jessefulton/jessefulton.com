Following Coinbase's major acquisition of Bison Trails, I served as Product Manager owning the developer platform, digital marketing properties, and cross-protocol developer primitives.

### The Ground Truth: Staking Fortress to Developer Platform
Bison Trails began as a premier, white-glove staking infrastructure platform safeguarding over **$30 billion in staked crypto assets**. My remit was to transform this institutional staking fortress into an open, self-serve developer platform offering standardized cryptographic primitives across 25+ wildly divergent blockchain protocols—all while navigating rigorous public-company compliance, legal guardrails, and security audits.

### Post-Acquisition Rebrand & Digital Operations
As the de facto product manager owning everything outside low-level node implementations:
- **Full Digital Property Rebrand:** I directed the end-to-end migration and rebranding of all digital properties from Bison Trails into Coinbase Cloud, consolidating marketing sites, developer documentation, and martech stacks.
- **Cross-Chain Primitives:** I collaborated with protocol engineers to define unified APIs and SDKs so that developers experienced consistent behavior across disparate blockchains.
- **Cultural M&A Navigation:** I acted as the cultural bridge as the first hire to complete official Coinbase onboarding within the acquired business unit, navigating complex organizational friction during rapid corporate integration.
- **Crypto-Winter Resilience:** I navigated intense macroeconomic market contractions and platform consolidation during the 2022 crypto winter, gathering battle-tested lessons in platform longevity and venture lifecycle management.
