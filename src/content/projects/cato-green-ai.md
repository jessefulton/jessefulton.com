At Cato Digital, we challenged the hyperscaler status quo by commercializing the circular economy for high-compute silicon, rescuing enterprise GPU servers from wasteful decommissioning cycles and converting them into ultra-low-cost bare-metal capacity for AI training and batch inference.

## 01 // The Strategic Context & Organizational Fragmentation
While the venture world was setting billions on fire waiting for 18-month lead times on brand-new H100s, tier-one enterprises and datacenters were decommissioning massive fleets of underutilized, battle-tested NVIDIA DGX supercomputers at pennies on the dollar. Hyperscalers charge a 10x premium for the illusion of hardware infallibility, even though real system resilience lives in the orchestration layer and distributed application mesh, not pristine silicon.

## 02 // The Unifying Framework & Alignment Model
* **Venture Unit Economics & Capacity Pricing:** As Chief of Staff and Head of Product/Infrastructure Strategy, I modeled the venture economics balancing power draw (kW/rack), cooling overhead (PUE), real estate square-footage, and capital depreciation.
* **Power-Distribution SLA Tiering:** Designed variable pricing models tied directly to power-distribution SLAs—enabling AI teams running fault-tolerant training runs and batch inference to purchase raw compute at a fraction of AWS/GCP rates.

## 03 // Cross-Functional Execution
Bridging physical datacenter operations, venture board alignment, and low-level network topology was an intense operational crucible:
* **The 24-Hour Supercomputer Rack:** Directed the off-grid turnup of **72 liquid-cooled NVIDIA DGX-2 supercomputers** in Iowa in under 24 hours. We calculated structural floor load limits, engineered high-density PDUs, configured 100GbE InfiniBand fabrics, and unified the nodes over NVSwitch into a high-throughput cluster capable of training foundation models.
* **Investor & Commercial Roadshows:** Built the strategic commercial narratives and financial models aligning infrastructure investors, carbon-offset certifiers, and AI research startups.

## 04 // Commercial & Venture Outcomes
Cato proved that circular computing could profitably deliver frontier AI compute while radically reducing datacenter carbon footprints and e-waste. The strategic models and bare-metal orchestration playbooks established a clear blueprint for sustainable, sovereign AI infrastructure.
