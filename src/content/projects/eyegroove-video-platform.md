Eyegroove was a visionary mobile creative platform allowing users to record 30-second music videos augmented with real-time interactive shaders, gesture-driven filter transformations, and audio-reactive effects.

### Intellectual Property & Patent Portfolio
The foundational technologies engineered at Eyegroove resulted in **6 issued US Utility Patents** covering touch-based creative tools, non-destructive video filtering, real-time media generation, and metadata persistence:
1. **[US10031921](https://patentimages.storage.googleapis.com/79/7f/89/eddaa4e967dd7b/US10031921.pdf)**: *Methods and systems for storage of media item metadata*
2. **[US10002642](https://patentimages.storage.googleapis.com/4d/9d/8e/0797db550ad9eb/US10002642.pdf)**: *Methods and devices for generating media items*
3. **[US9268787](https://patentimages.storage.googleapis.com/44/84/f5/f17376b186928e/US9268787.pdf)**: *Methods and devices for synchronizing and sharing media items*
4. **[US9207857](https://patentimages.storage.googleapis.com/cb/31/8d/9e75b31776b79c/US9207857.pdf)**: *Methods and devices for presenting interactive media items*
5. **[US9207844](https://patentimages.storage.googleapis.com/76/f7/bf/9e0762caa6e457/US9207844.pdf)**: *Methods and devices for touch-based media creation*
6. **[US9116912](https://patentimages.storage.googleapis.com/b6/b2/05/30b15e82006751/US9116912.pdf)**: *Methods and devices for modifying pre-existing media items*

### Acquisition & System Scale
In 2016, Eyegroove was acquired by Facebook (Meta), where its patent-backed video interaction paradigms and real-time shader pipelines directly influenced the development of Instagram Stories, Reels, and Meta AR camera effects.
