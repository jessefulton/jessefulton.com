The Wildlife Tracker is a social research game developed in conjunction with the Environmental Studies department and the Digital Arts + New Media Program at UCSC.

The platform challenges players to correctly identify wildlife species in field camera trap photographs, generating confidence ratings that automate the indexing of ecological migration and biodiversity datasets.
