Created for Activision's *Call of Duty: Infinite Warfare*, *Terminal Tours (Hostile Takeover)* was a pioneering multimodal conversational alternate-reality campaign built inside Facebook Messenger, engaging millions of fans globally in cryptographic military espionage.

## 01 // The Production Frontier & Regulatory Stakes
In 2016, conversational bots were largely rudimentary text responders. Activision needed a high-concurrency, multimodal storytelling engine capable of withstanding viral traffic spikes from the *Call of Duty* community without crashing, dropping dialogue state, or exposing narrative spoilers ahead of global launch.

## 02 // Systems Architecture & Data Plumbing
* **Deterministic High-Concurrency State Machine:** Architected complex narrative decision trees and natural language intent matching on the PullString conversational platform.
* **Multimodal Asset Streaming Pipeline:** Engineered dynamic asset delivery pipelines returning synchronized classified surveillance stills, encrypted audio stems, and episodic video clips based on player dialogue branches and cryptographic puzzle solutions.
* **Adversarial Error Handling & Easter Eggs:** Designed comprehensive fallback handlers turning conversational failures into immersive storytelling moments, anticipating adversarial user prompts with in-universe Easter eggs.

## 03 // The Engineering Crucible
At launch, the *Call of Duty* player base overwhelmed our endpoints, generating over **6 million interactive sessions in the first 24 hours**. Hardcore gaming communities on Reddit and Discord coordinated mass deciphering attacks against our cryptographic puzzles. We had to dynamically scale webhook capacity, tune rate-limiting thresholds, and patch state-transition logic in real time as players solved multi-stage ARG puzzles in hours instead of days.

## 04 // Hard Performance & Defensibility
The project processed over 6M sessions with zero catastrophic downtime, earning a **Cannes Gold Lion for Innovative Use of Social**, **ADC Gold Cube**, multiple **Shorty Awards**, and the **Project Isaac Gold Award for Gaming Invention**. The deterministic state-machine principles and adversarial conversational guardrails developed here directly inform modern agentic evaluation frameworks.
