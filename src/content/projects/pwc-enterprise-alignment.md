PwC Sightline is a proprietary enterprise tax platform used by PricewaterhouseCoopers to ensure regulatory compliance and drive corporate tax strategies.

### From Discovery Sprint to Strategic Anchor
Starting from an initial six-week visioning engagement, Jesse facilitated high-impact design sprints and executive workshopping sessions that unblocked competing internal priorities across tax leadership, security, and global practice leaders.

### Key Deliverables & Outcomes
- **Sightline Mobile:** Defined product requirements and oversaw end-to-end design and delivery of the executive companion mobile application.
- **Strategic Prioritization Frameworks:** Established research-backed roadmaps and quantitative feature scoring models for platform product owners.
- **Account Expansion:** Built high-trust relationships with client partners, expanding the initial advisory sprint into the agency's primary enterprise relationship.
