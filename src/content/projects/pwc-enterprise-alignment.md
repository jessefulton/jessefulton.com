PwC Sightline is the flagship enterprise technology platform powering PricewaterhouseCoopers' corporate tax practice, managing compliance, audit risk, and strategic forecasting for multinational clients representing over $50B in taxable assets.

## 01 // The Strategic Context & Organizational Fragmentation
PwC’s corporate tax ecosystem was heavily siloed across 8 distinct business units (Fortune 10 corporations, sovereign funds, energy conglomerates, private wealth). Each practice operated on disparate legacy desktop tools and custom spreadsheets. Leadership needed a unified 3–5 year platform roadmap, but Senior Equity Partners were deeply skeptical of outside consultants pitching generic design frameworks without understanding tax law or financial mechanics.

## 02 // The Unifying Framework & Alignment Model
* **The Repeatable 4-Week Roadshow Playbook:** I engineered a disciplined, repeatable executive discovery and co-creation framework:
  1. *Week 1 (Discovery):* Deep stakeholder immersion and frontline CPA workflow auditing.
  2. *Week 2 (Synthesis & Modeling):* Mapping fragmented workflows into unified platform schemas.
  3. *Week 3 (Executive Co-Creation Workshop):* Moderating intensive stakeholder workshops with Partners and VPs.
  4. *Week 4 (Rapid Prototyping & Validation):* Delivering interactive clickable prototypes and executive alignment decks.
* **Speaking in Spreadsheets:** Replaced standard UX jargon with concrete financial unit economics, billable-hour recovery models, and risk-reduction metrics that directly aligned with partner equity incentives.

## 03 // Cross-Functional Execution
Over an 11-month period, we executed this roadshow framework across 8 separate business units, conducting **100+ executive and practitioner interviews** and building **50+ interactive prototypes**. We navigated complex internal firm politics, turning skeptical practice leaders into active champions by rapidly translating their daily spreadsheet frustrations into tangible software prototypes within 72 hours.

## 04 // Commercial & Venture Outcomes
The strategic framework transformed what began as a **$60,000 discovery workshop into YML / Code & Theory’s #1 account by revenue in under 12 months** (scaling to multi-million-dollar multi-year product engineering engagements). The resulting product roadmap unified PwC Sightline into a cohesive enterprise SaaS ecosystem, dramatically reducing manual audit friction across the firm.
