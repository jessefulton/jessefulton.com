At Thrive AI Health, I established the foundational agent orchestration systems, evaluation harnesses, and real-time voice architectures powering autonomous conversational healthcare interactions.

### Building Zero-to-One Without an Industry Playbook
Deploying autonomous agents into clinical healthcare environments meant operating in territory with zero established industry standards. Our engineering mandate required balancing strict medical and legal compliance with the inherent non-determinism of large language models. The hardest challenge was not merely preventing hallucinations—it was engineering **audit-ready agentic transparency**, allowing healthcare compliance officers to inspect *why* every clinical decision was made without violating strict HIPAA data retention and patient privacy invariants.

### Multimodal Real-Time Voice & Dynamic Evals
Acting across platform architecture, product incubation, and forward-deployed engineering:
- **Dynamic Multilingual Medical Interviews:** I engineered an autonomous agent pipeline capable of conducting personalized medical interviews in multiple languages, fetching clinical context and dynamic medical topics on-the-fly.
- **Real-Time Voice Agent Layer:** We layered sub-second voice interaction atop the agent framework, maintaining natural conversational freedom while executing continuous safety guardrail enforcement.
- **Deterministic Evaluation Harness:** I built automated synthetic eval suites and regression test matrices that simulated dynamic conversational branches, accelerating our org-wide engineering velocity by 5x.
- **Audit-Ready Runtimes:** I designed structured memory management (balancing short-term context and long-term recall) with transparent reasoning traces that satisfied strict regulatory oversight.
