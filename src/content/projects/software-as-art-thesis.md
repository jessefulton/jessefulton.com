## Overview

Completed Master of Fine Arts (MFA) thesis research within the prestigious Digital Arts and New Media (DANM) program at UC Santa Cruz. 

The research explored computational aesthetics, generative systems, and the evolving role of code as a primary expressive and cultural medium, forming the philosophical foundation for a career bridging deep engineering with human-centered creative design.
