Completed as part of the Digital Arts & New Media (DANM) MFA program at UC Santa Cruz, *Software as Art* explored the philosophical and practical intersections between computational algorithms and human expressive gesture.

### Autonomous Drawing Systems
The thesis investigated software not merely as a functional utility, but as an expressive, generative medium capable of autonomous artistic output and collaborative drawing performance.
